Connection

The extension injects the preset toolbar and applies your templates inside Ghost Admin. Enter the URL you use to open it — with or without a subdirectory (for example https://ghost.example.com or https://example.com/blog). Access is requested only for that exact installation's /ghost/* path and only after you click “Enable”. No other sites are touched, and no long-lived secret is stored.
